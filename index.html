📖 How to use SpeechLens

Using SpeechLens is ridiculously simple:

  1. Open SpeechLens. You'll see a clean, minimal window.
  2. Select your input device (microphone) and your output device (headphones / speakers) from the dropdowns.
  3. Toggle "Noise Removal" on. You can adjust the intensity with the slider — from "Light" to "Aggressive".
  4. Press "Start Listening" and speak. SpeechLens will process your mic in real-time, sending the enhanced audio to any app — Zoom, Teams, QuickTime, Audacity, you name it.

Also try "Voice Isolation" mode for maximum focus on your voice, or "Enhance Recording" to clean up audio files you've already captured. Just drag & drop a file into SpeechLens and away you go.
